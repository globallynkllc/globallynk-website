FAQ

Which blend is best for wet wipes?

More viscose gives a softer, more absorbent wipe; more polyester adds strength and lowers cost. Around 40–50 gsm is common for wet wipes.

Can you supply plastic-free wipes fabric?

Yes — 100% viscose or cotton spunlace for biodegradable wipes.

Do you supply finished wipes?

We can source finished and packed wipes on request, under your brand.

What width should I order?

Tell us your converting machine's width and we will supply slit rolls to match.
