FAQ

PET or PP geotextile — which should I use?

Polypropylene is lighter and more resistant to chemicals; polyester handles higher temperatures and long-term load better. Your project specification usually decides.

What weight is used for road separation?

Often 150–400 gsm, depending on the design. Always follow the engineer's specification; we will source to it and supply the test reports.

Can you provide geotextile test reports?

Yes — tensile strength, CBR puncture and permeability from the mill or a third-party lab.

Do you supply rolls for automotive use?

Yes, to the customer's specification for weight, thickness, color and any flame-retardant requirement.
