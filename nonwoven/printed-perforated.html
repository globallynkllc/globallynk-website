FAQ

Can you print our logo?

Yes. Send your artwork; we confirm a printed proof with you before production.

Is there a minimum for printed fabric?

Printing needs plates set up for each design, so custom prints have a higher minimum than plain fabric. Tell us your quantity and we will confirm.

What sizes do tablecloths come in?

Common rectangular and round sizes, or rolls perforated at the length you choose.

Can you supply finished tablecloths?

Yes — pre-cut and packed, ready for retail or catering.
