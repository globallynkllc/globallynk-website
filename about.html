Who we are

We are not a mill, and we're not a middleman who disappears after the invoice. We are the link between the factories that make fabric and the businesses that turn it into products.

GlobalLynk LLC is an international general trading and ecommerce company registered in Sheridan, Wyoming, USA. Today we trade nonwoven fabrics, electric mountain bikes and genuine luxury auto spare parts: we source from qualified suppliers and deliver to converters, manufacturers, distributors and retailers — wherever they are.

We don't manufacture anything ourselves. We buy from vetted suppliers, match each buyer with the right one for the specification, check the goods before they ship, and handle the export paperwork and freight — so you get consistent fabric without managing factories yourself.

GlobalLynk is a sister company of Athena General Trading and shares its sourcing network and export know-how.

How it works

Four steps from inquiry to arrival.

A simple, documented process, whether you're ordering trial rolls or a full container of fabric.

01

Send your spec

Fabric type, GSM, width, color, treatment, quantity and destination. Samples welcome.

02

Receive a quote

Itemized pricing, lead time and a freight estimate, with samples or data sheets on request.

03

Confirm & we source

We place the order with the right supplier, check GSM, width and roll quality, and prepare the documents.

04

Ship & track

Rolls are loaded for container efficiency and shipped with tracking and full paperwork.
