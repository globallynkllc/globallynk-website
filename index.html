Who we are

We are not a manufacturer, and we're not a single-product trader. We are the link between the factories that make things and the businesses that need them.

GlobalLynk LLC is an international general trading and ecommerce company registered in Sheridan, Wyoming, USA. We source products from vetted manufacturers and move them to distributors, wholesalers, retailers and institutional buyers — wherever they are.

GlobalLynk is a sister company of Athena General Trading, and shares its sourcing network, quality process and export know-how. That gives buyers a US-registered trading partner with an established supply chain behind it.

Our catalog spans automotive spare parts, electric mountain bikes and industrial nonwoven fabrics today, with baby care products next — and we source other goods on request.

How it works

Four steps from inquiry to arrival.

A simple, documented process — whether you're ordering a pallet of filters or a container of e-bikes.

01

Send your inquiry

Tell us the product, quantity and destination. Part numbers or specs help us move faster.

02

Receive a quote

We come back with itemized pricing, availability, lead time and a logistics estimate.

03

Confirm & we process

Agree terms and confirm. We handle procurement, quality checks and export documentation.

04

Ship & track

Your order ships with tracking. We coordinate freight and the customs paperwork.
