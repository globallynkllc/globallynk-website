Why GlobalLynk

Consistent fabric, roll after roll.

Our approach is simple: honest sourcing, transparent pricing, and delivery you can plan your production around.

01 / Specification

Sourced to your spec

We find suppliers who can match your GSM, width, color, treatment and roll length — not just whatever is in stock.

02 / Quality

Checked before loading

GSM, width, tensile strength and roll condition verified, with photos and third-party inspection if you want it.

03 / Transparency

Line-item pricing

Detailed quotes with all variables included. No hidden markups and no surprise fees at shipping.

04 / Flexibility

Your trade terms

FOB, CIF, CFR, DDP and more. Payment by T/T, letter of credit or D/P depending on the relationship.

05 / Paperwork

Complete documentation

Commercial invoice, packing list, certificate of origin, bill of lading and test reports — prepared for your country.

06 / Reliability

Proactive updates

Committed timelines. If something changes, you hear it from us before you need to ask.
