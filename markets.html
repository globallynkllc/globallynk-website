Quality & documents

Five checks before anything ships.

Every order goes through the same quality-control process, and ships with a complete, checked document set.

Stage 01Supplier qualification

Background verification, authorization checks, factory capability assessment, reference checks and sample evaluation before the first bulk order.

Stage 02Order-level verification

Part-number and specification cross-check, visual inspection, random sampling for dimensional and functional checks, and quantity reconciliation.

Stage 03Pre-shipment inspection

Full carton count, packing-list verification, product condition check, labeling accuracy and photographs for your review.

Stage 04Third-party inspection

Buyer-appointed inspections are welcome. We coordinate with SGS, Bureau Veritas, TÜV, Intertek and others before loading.

Stage 05Documentation review

All export documents checked for accuracy, HS codes verified, and the full set cross-checked against your LC or import requirements.
